# Historical tracking archive

That local, gitignored tracker and its linked task graph, decisions, coverage,
issues, and evidence are canonical. See ADR-0009.

and research can still be checked. Do not update them for new work or seed an
implementation queue from their old `planned` rows. Their recommendations,
counts, priorities, and verification claims may be stale.

recommendations have not been adopted. The tracking migration leaves it
